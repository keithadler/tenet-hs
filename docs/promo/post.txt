1/ tenet-hs: an independent Lean 4 kernel in Haskell. Free, Apache 2.0.

It re-checks all of Init. Give every theorem another's proof and it rejects the same 41,378 that Tenet does.

More independent kernels is better. I'll maintain both.

github.com/keithadler/tenet-hs

2/ It reads lean4export files and re-checks every declaration from scratch: 57,919 in Init, none rejected. It re-derives every constructor and recursor, nested and mutual inductives included, and compares them field for field with what the exporter wrote.

3/ Its fuzzer found a real bug on the first run: 9 of 120 damaged files split it from Tenet. All one mistake in how it handled a rejected inductive. Fixed. Now 350 variants agree on every rejection.

4/ It is slower than Tenet, two to five times on the arena's performance tests, and the docs say so, with the profiles and the hash-consing attempt that made it ten times worse. It builds with plain GHC and needs no packages.

5/ Not planting a flag. I will maintain it with Tenet and the rest of the family, because every independent kernel is another chance to find a bug in the reference, in the others, or in the spec they all chase.
